## 🔍 Troubleshooting

* **❌ Video not being detected?** Make sure you have clicked play on the video. The extension needs the player to initialize before it can grab the source.
* **📉 Only seeing one quality option?** The extension can only display the resolutions that SpankBang makes available for that specific video.
* **🔄 Grabbing the wrong stream?** Simply refresh the webpage, start playback again, and reopen the downloader interface.
* **🛑 Download fails mid-way?** This is usually caused by a temporary disruption in your internet connection. You can quickly retry the download directly from the extension popup.
* **🔐 Can't download restricted videos?** The extension only works on media you can already open and play in your active browser session.
